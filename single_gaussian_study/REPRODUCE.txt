ANALYSIS AND INTERPRETATION

results.csv has means plus paired context/initialization-seed uncertainty for
joint/K/W/goal rates, before/after raw action MSE, total regularizedJ, first-
replan improvement and failure diagnostics. seed_results.csv shows every seed.
paired_recovery_regression.csv compares actual matched N0/native outcomes to
each N>0, reporting recoveries, regressions and paired net changes. Bootstrap
resamples64contexts and3seed indices, keeping methods paired,2000times.
Context-only intervals are also supplied. Only3 seeds and one frozen model
pair are available; reused research banks and sparse groups limit inference.
Later/episode-mean losses reflect differing closed-loop observations/replan
counts; the first-replan comparison holds observed inputs and initial codes
fixed. Raw action agreement and totalJ are separate throughout.

initialization_sensitivity.csv gives descriptive raw-Gaussian-magnitude bins
and sampled sign branches. These groups do not constitute extra independent
contexts and are never used for selecting an initialization or result.
historical_grid_reference.csv reuses the completed original grid study, with
its exact summary hashes, rather than rerunning or retuning that method.
Native examples are outcome-selected illustrations, not success estimates.
All variable paths/case geometry are rasterized inside PDF axes on the GPU;
Cube projections do not replace the original strict3D physical monitor.

Raw NPZs/observations/actions/poses/Gaussian values per episode/planning traces
and individual records stay on the GPU. export.py transfers only whitelisted
aggregate statistics, cell-level checks, figures, frozen configs and source.
No public repository or previous study output is changed.
