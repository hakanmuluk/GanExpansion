\topic{Controlled setup}
The same frozen base and independent keep-out/waypoint actors predict 16 actions
from the current observation and their original skill context. Sample one pair
$u_K,u_W\sim\mathcal N(0,1)$ per episode, project to the original bounds and
permitted signs, and initialize $a_K=a_W=|b_1|$ from the supplied original base
style. TwoRoom retains its negative branches; Cube retains the sampled signs.
Code magnitudes lie in $[.01,2.5]$. The original bounded logit/sigmoid fraction
clamp $[.001,.999]$ applies to every $N$, including zero, so all settings use
exactly the same usable starting point. $N$ counts updates at \emph{every}
replan. Each replan starts only from the
previous selected codes, without further sampling. Adam moments reset per
replan; learning rate $.12$ and best-iterate retention along that \emph{one}
trajectory are unchanged. There is no grid, top-$k$, restart or best-seed choice.
\[
 J=\underbrace{\frac1{8d}\sum_{t=1}^{8}\|A_K(t)-A_W(t)\|_2^2}_{L_{\rm agree}}
 +\frac{\lambda_a}{2}\sum_{j\in\{K,W\}}(a_j-|b_1|)^2
 +\frac{.01}{2}(u_K^2+u_W^2).
\]
$\lambda_a=1$ / $.0005$ and $d=2$ / 5 in TwoRoom/Cube; the optional encoder
cycle is disabled. All neural models and other coefficients remain frozen.
Both execute 8 actions and re-observe: TwoRoom issues
$\operatorname{clip}(A_W+A_K-A_B)$; Cube issues $A_W$ with its original gripper
threshold. Their different executors and strict success monitors are preserved.

\topic{Runtime and initialization sensitivity}
The uncontended batch1 benchmark uses 12 contexts/environment, all three seeds,
initial/midpoint N20 observations and three repeats (216 calls/setting).
Observed states and warm previous-code inputs match across $N$. Cold calls use
production Gaussian sampling with common probe seeds; batch1 draws need not
match TwoRoom's native batch64 RNG layout. Each call uses $2(N+2)$ actor-forward
sample equivalents, one base forward and $N$ input backwards.

\topic{Evidence and limits}
All 90 cells (5,760 evaluations) use 384 reused contexts and three seeds;
@@AUDIT@@ were checked for one initial draw, no grid/restart, unchanged bounds,
objective and warm starts, and shared initial optimizer prefixes. Every TwoRoom
path matched original-native replay; Cube ran in MuJoCo. All 64 parallel Cube0
paths matched an excluded serial pilot, whose validator-default bug was fixed
without changing the planner.

Intervals resample contexts and initialization seeds independently, keeping
methods paired, 2,000 times. Context-only and seed-conditional Wilson intervals
are also supplied. Three seeds, one frozen model pair and previously used banks
limit inference. Empirical intervals can collapse with no observed variation.
Historical-grid contrasts combine initialization and one-versus-four-start
differences. Full $J$ includes regularization; later/episode-mean losses also
reflect differing observations. The results do not isolate the agreement term.
Code/configs, hashes and editable source accompany the report; native arrays
and individual records remain on the GPU.
