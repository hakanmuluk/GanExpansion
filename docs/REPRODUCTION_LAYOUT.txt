The public checkout provides portable examples and preserves the original public
asset paths. New frozen composition weights install under research_assets/.

Exact completed-study reruns use canonical /content layouts recorded by the
scientific descriptors. The v1.1.0 single-gaussian-reproduction archive includes
the exact original source/configuration snapshot and original checkpoint bytes.
Extract it into an unused execution root and follow its REPRODUCE.txt; restore or
regenerate the six protected banks and check their recorded hashes first.
Do not unpack a canonical research snapshot over a working public checkout.

The public checkout retains existing release compatibility edits: postponed
Python annotations, published discriminator initialization paths and legacy CPU
CLI support. These do not change the frozen models' numerical inference. Exact
source-hash checks must use the supplied canonical source snapshot instead.

The base/adapter training study has its own ablation_study/REPRODUCE.txt and
recorded configurations. Its adapter-rank study uses published original bases;
its broader-data Cube base study and current composition use later Cube weights.
Keep checkpoint families and their training/evaluation banks separate.
Full training needs the input archives named in those notes, in addition to
selected inference weights. Raw observations/actions/poses are not distributed.
